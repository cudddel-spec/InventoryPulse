1.0.2
- Events are only registered while a feature needs them (BagCount, StackCount, bank mute, HUD only)
  and are unregistered as soon as it is switched off
- Inventory events filtered by bag and update reason (no work on durability, dye or charge changes)
- Only one global variable (InventoryPulse)
- Settings are saved per server (EU, NA, PTS); existing settings are copied to each server once
- AI disclosure at the top of the description

1.0.1
- StackCount also reports full 200 stacks in the backpack (potions, soul gems, lockpicks ...),
  with ESO Plus in addition to the craft bag; can be switched off
- BagCount: optional percent display ("75% full"), with or without word, colors at 80/90/95 %

1.0.0
- First release / Erste Veröffentlichung
- BagCount: used/free backpack slots as flying text, with fill level and step filter
- StackCount: message when a craft bag material reaches a multiple of 200 (ESO Plus)
- Optional: font size grows with fill level, shrink during flight, sound for StackCount
- Settings menu (LibAddonMenu-2.0, optional) and full chat control (/ipulse help)
- English and German
